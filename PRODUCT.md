# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary user: the **firm owner / managing partner** — the person who oversees the whole firm. They run the dashboard, watch the matters pipeline, make approvals (leaves, expenses), and read firm-level metrics. The dashboard and firm-level views are built for them.

Other audiences, confirmed by the role model in code:

- **OWNER** — the person who owns/runs the firm (see primary user above): full, unlimited access to the firm — matters, tasks, associates & staff, attendance, leaves, expenses.
- **ADMIN** — expenses & billing **only**. Stripped from everything else: no matters, tasks, associates, attendance, or leaves.
- **ASSOCIATE** — lawyers and staff: self-serve attendance check-in/out, leave applications, their own assigned tasks, and access **only** to the matters/cases they are a part of.
- **SUPER_ADMIN** — platform operator: manages tenant firms (create firm, set owner credentials), outside any single firm.

## Product Purpose

LGA is a "legal terminal" — one operational system for running a law firm, covering court/case work plus firm back-office (associates & staff, attendance & leaves, expenses & billing). It currently serves **Laal Global Advisory**, a Pakistani law firm, and is deliberately architected (multi-tenant) so other law firms can be onboarded as tenants later.

Success for the owner/partner: the full operating picture of the firm — active matters, hearing calendar, attendance board, spend — answerable from one screen, with court work and firm ops in a single loop rather than separate tools.

## Positioning

The differentiator is **Pakistani court procedure specificity**. The matter pipeline is modeled on the actual CPC/CrPC stage flow — civil and criminal stages seeded with Urdu legal terminology (Jawab Dawa, Tanqeehat, Behas, Faisla, Tareekh-e-Pesh, Fard-e-Jurm), Pakistani courts and benches, CNR and FIR numbers, and next-hearing-date (Tareekh) tracking. A general practice-management tool could not truthfully copy this stage/terminology specificity; it is built for how Pakistani litigation actually runs.

## Operating Context

- **Pakistani legal system** is the day-to-day reality: district & sessions courts, High Court benches, Civil Courts; court case numbers ("Civil Suit No. 452/2025"), CNR records, FIR numbers; hearing dates referred to as Tareekh-e-Pesh.
- **Attendance** comes from three sources: Hikvision biometric devices (firm-owned), remote self check-in/out, and manual admin records; a firm-wide attendance board shows present/absent/on-leave/remote.
- **Court-stage workflow**: matters progress through court stages; hearings are scheduled with purpose, presiding judge, attendees, next date + next purpose; tasks are assigned around hearing prep (document filing, printing/binding, client follow-up, witness briefing, legal research).
- **Admin rituals**: leave requests with an approver workflow and per-year balances; expense logging (fixed + manual) with recurring templates.
- **Tenant model**: each law firm is a tenant (Firm); a SUPER_ADMIN provisions firms from the platform area. Current posture is internal-first with the architecture ready for outside tenants.
- Dark and light themes are supported throughout (next-themes).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Auth**: JWT + refresh tokens (bcrypt password hashes), roles SUPER_ADMIN / OWNER / ADMIN / ASSOCIATE, `mustChangePassword` and `isActive` flags, email+password only (no external SSO/OAuth).
- **Associates & Staff**: HR profiles with salary, designation, department, employment type (FULL_TIME/CONTRACT/PARTNER), work mode (ONSITE/REMOTE/HYBRID), joining date, status, reporting-manager hierarchy, optional Hikvision user ID.
- **Attendance & Leaves**: attendance records (PRESENT/ABSENT/HALF_DAY/LEAVE) from BIOMETRIC_IMPORT / REMOTE_CHECKIN / MANUAL_ADMIN; leave types with annual allotment + carry-forward; leave requests (PENDING/APPROVED/REJECTED) decided by an approver; per-year leave balances.
- **Expenses & Billing**: expenses typed FIXED or MANUAL with category, description, vendor, payment method, receipt URL; recurring expense templates with MONTHLY/QUARTERLY/ANNUALLY cycles and auto-generation (`isAutoGenerated`).
- **Matters & Cases**: firm case number, court case number, CNR, case type (CIVIL/CRIMINAL/WRIT/FAMILY/SERVICE/CORPORATE/TAXATION), court, bench, presiding judge, current court stage, status (ACTIVE/ARCHIVED/DECIDED/CLOSED); parties with roles (plaintiff, defendant, petitioner, accused, opposing counsel, witness, court clerk, …); hearings (SCHEDULED/HELD/ADJOURNED/SINE_DIE/DECIDED) with attendees and order-sheet/proceedings fields; tasks with type/status/priority/due date/estimated hours/notes/attachments; versioned case documents by category; audit log of stage changes and key actions.
- **Terminology is bilingual**: Urdu legal terms are used in the product alongside English; future work should keep the Urdu terms (they are the point).
- **Currency undecided**: seed salaries read as PKR (180,000 / 100,000 / 80,000), while dashboard copy currently shows USD ("$150,200"). Neither is confirmed; future work must not assume one.
- **Dashboard metrics are placeholders**: the associate headcount (128) and billing figures ($150,200) on the dashboard are hardcoded sample values, not live data.

## Product Principles

1. **Court-procedure specificity is the moat.** Preserve and deepen the CPC/CrPC stage modeling and Urdu legal terminology; never genericize it to fit a generic practice-management template.
2. **The owner/partner sees the whole firm first.** Firm-level dashboard, pipeline, and approvals are built for the person running the firm — decisions, not data entry.
3. **One terminal, one operating loop.** Court work and firm back-office are the same product; modules should reinforce the all-in-one loop, not feel like separate tools bolted together.
4. **Tenant-ready from the first commit.** No single-firm hardcoding that would block onboarding another law firm when that stage arrives.
5. **No fabricated facts.** Sample/placeholder figures and fictional seed cases are visibly what they are; real numbers, testimonials, and customer claims only when they exist.
